# Onyx — Precision Sprint (truth engines · movement library · Body tab · écorché atlas · programs & goals · wrist vitals)


## Context
Onyx 9.0.0 closed the UI/UX Overhaul (Stone material, 8 stones, bento summary, Glance watch, Replay share). The founder now reports 20 defects and asks: the live-workout "Add a movement" list is an endless flat list; the treadmill card pre-fills a fake "1 km / 10 min"; set-row weights render at two sizes; the muscle atlas looks like white plastic; a 50 kg × 7 row won one PR instead of two; the post-workout summary and banners still scroll; the RPE screen is generic and suggests HR/kcal without a watch; Onyx tonnage (4409 kg) disagrees with Hevy (4372.8 kg); the timer sheet is grandiose; unticked sets need a clean "ghost" semantics; total-sets must count warm-ups and cardio; no in-app program builder; no goal-linked nutrition setup; Train's "Last: 45 kg × 9 @ 9.0" line is ugly; the background is flat black on phone and watch; Replay share freezes and shares an empty image; cholesterol reads 610 mg after one omelet; Pulse has no clear purpose; the watch needs vitals, complications, food/water sync, a title that fits, and a six-petal Glance.

Exploration (three read-only agents + graphify, 2026-09-25) found the code truth behind every item; the founder answered 24 questions and approved 12 design proposals, the Écorché atlas, and a 6-lane plan run **three lanes at a time**. Final version at close-out: **10.0.0** (ghost/set-count semantics, tonnage basis, program schema are user-visible).

Roles: **Fable 5.1 (High)** = architect, prompt author, Lane C builder (the math lane), final reviewer. **Opus 5.5 (Extra High)** = builder of every other lane.
